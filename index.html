Industry context: where my software is used

My applications are used across Bosch and by carmakers such as Volkswagen, BMW and Daimler at the final stage of car software development. Once software for a car's internal computers is tested and approved, teams use our tools to track each release, archive it and hand it on to plants.

We release new versions of the tools every PI (program increment) and whenever a new requirement comes in. Reliability matters: the right software version has to reach the right place, every time.

ECU
A small computer in a car that controls one system, like the engine or brakes.
PVER
A finished, approved software release for an ECU.
OEM
The carmaker, such as VW, BMW or Daimler. OEM teams use our tools too.
